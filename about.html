OUR STORY

From a LAN party to a full arena

Next Level began as a weekend LAN party hosted by three friends in a converted warehouse space. What started as bring-your-own-rig sessions for 20 people grew into a full esports venue with private suites, tournament infrastructure and a resident host team.

Today we run everything from solo VIP sessions to 120-player corporate tournaments, but the standard hasn't changed: real hardware, real support, zero compromises on latency.
