ABOUT US

Welcome to Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena, where gaming, competition and entertainment come together.

WHO WE ARE

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a gaming and entertainment destination based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, Next Level was created to provide gamers with a modern space where they can play, compete, train and enjoy immersive gaming experiences.

From gaming packages and esports events to individual experiences, our goal is to create an exciting environment for gamers, friends and groups.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to create memorable gaming experiences through quality facilities, exciting activities and a welcoming environment for gamers of all levels.
